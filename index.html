⚡ LIVE WARNINGS

    About this simulation

    Stock & tonnage

    Content is measured in tonnes (t), litres for diesel. Stock integrates fill − discharge continuously. Each 40 m silo holds 32 segments × 10 t = 320 t.

    Temperature & moisture

    Values drift toward the ambient day-cycle. Moist, full silos self-heat; aeration cools the grain and dries it toward its target.

    Diesel & generator

    The tank uses true horizontal-cylinder geometry (Ø1.5 m × L3.0 m ≈ 5 301 L). The generator draws 25 L/h while switched on, plus a tiny standby loss.

    All values are simulated, not for operations.
